Who We Are

ZB Creative ("we," "us," "our") operates the website zbcreative.agency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out a contact form, request a quote, or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. GA4 collects information such as your device type, browser type, operating system, approximate geographic location (city/region level, not precise location), pages visited, time spent on pages, and how you arrived at our site (referral source). GA4 uses cookies to distinguish unique users and sessions. Google Analytics does not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information unless you provide it through a form on our site. For more information on how Google processes this data, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies: Our site uses cookies, which are small text files stored on your device. These include first-party cookies necessary for site functionality and third-party cookies from Google Analytics used for website performance analysis. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • To analyze website traffic and usage patterns so we can improve our site's content, performance, and user experience
  • To communicate with you about our services if you have reached out to us
  • To maintain the security and functionality of our website

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may share information with the following categories of service providers who assist us in operating our website and business:

  • Analytics Providers: Google Analytics processes website usage data on our behalf. Google may use this data to improve its own services. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Hosting Providers: Our website hosting provider may process data as necessary to serve our website to you.

We may also disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and do not collect personal information.

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to collect anonymous usage data about how visitors interact with our site. These cookies help us understand which pages are most popular, how visitors navigate the site, and where visitors come from.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling cookies may affect your experience on our website.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you and how we use it
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the information provided below.

For Residents of Other US States with Privacy Laws

Several other states including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and others have enacted privacy laws granting residents similar rights to access, delete, and opt out of certain data processing. If you are a resident of a state with an applicable privacy law, you may exercise your rights by contacting us using the information below.

Global Privacy Control

We respect the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request for the sale or sharing of your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain our business records. Google Analytics data is retained according to our GA4 settings, which is currently set to 14 months.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
