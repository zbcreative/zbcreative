Your Right to Opt Out

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and similar laws in other US states, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information.

Our Current Practices

ZB Creative does not sell your personal information. We do not exchange your data for money or other valuable consideration.

We use Google Analytics on our website to understand how visitors use our site, which helps us improve our content and user experience. Under some state privacy law definitions, the use of third-party analytics cookies may be considered "sharing" of personal information. We want to be transparent about this.

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of analytics data collection on our website in the following ways:

Method 01

Browser-Based Opt-Out

Install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on. This prevents Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits to any website.

Method 02

Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor GPC signals sent by your browser. If you enable GPC in your browser or through a privacy-focused browser extension, we will treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sharing of your personal information.

You can learn more about GPC at globalprivacycontrol.org.

Method 03

Cookie Settings

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling analytics cookies will prevent Google Analytics from tracking your activity on our site.

Method 04

Contact Us Directly

If you would like to submit an opt-out request or have questions about your data, you can reach us at: zack@zbcreative.agency

What Happens When You Opt Out

If you opt out of analytics tracking:

✓
Your browsing activity on our site will no longer be collected by Google Analytics
✓
You will still have full access to our website and all of its content
✓
Your experience on our site will not be degraded or limited in any way
✓
We will not discriminate against you for exercising your opt-out rights

Verification

We do not require you to create an account or verify your identity to opt out of data sharing. Opting out through any of the methods above is sufficient.
